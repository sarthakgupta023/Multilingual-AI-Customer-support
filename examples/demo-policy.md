# Sundial Demo Services — Fictional Leave Policy

**Document ID:** DEMO-LEAVE-01

This policy is fictional and exists only for demonstrating the application. It does not describe a real employer or employee.

## Annual paid leave

Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated allowance based on their contracted working hours. Unused leave may be carried forward up to 5 days into the next calendar year.

## Requesting leave

Employees should submit a leave request to their manager at least 7 calendar days before the intended start date. Approval depends on the team's schedule. For emergencies, contact the manager as soon as possible.

## Help

For circumstances not covered by this demo policy, contact the human support team. The assistant cannot approve leave or change an employee's balance.
