### 2. Standard Pay-As-You-Go Rates (Without Pack)
If traveling without a GlobalPass pack, standard roaming charges apply:
- Outgoing call to India: ₹50 / minute.
- Local call in visited country: ₹30 / minute.
- Incoming calls: ₹30 / minute.
- Data usage: ₹500 / 10 MB (Data is blocked by default at ₹1,000 unbilled usage to protect from accidental bill shock).

---

### 3. Pre-Departure Activation Checklist
1. International Roaming service is **active by default** for all TeleNova prepaid and postpaid customers in India.
2. In device settings, turn on **Data Roaming** (`Settings > Mobile Network > Data Roaming: ON`).
3. If connecting to a local partner network fails upon arrival, select network manually from settings (e.g., AT&T/T-Mobile in USA, Vodafone/EE in UK, Etisalat/du in UAE).
4. For international support, WhatsApp TeleNova Global Concierge at **+91-99880-12345** (toll-free).
