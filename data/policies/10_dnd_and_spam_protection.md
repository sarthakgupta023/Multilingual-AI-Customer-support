# TeleNova Do Not Disturb (DND) & AI Spam Shield

## Document ID: TN-POL-10
**Last Updated:** October 2026  
**Applicability:** All TeleNova Prepaid & Postpaid Subscribers

---

### 1. Activating National Do Not Disturb (DND)
To stop promotional and telemarketing phone calls or SMS:
- **Fully Blocked Category:** Send SMS `START 0` to **1909**, or call IVR **1909** and select Option 1.
- **Selective Blocking Categories:**
  - `START 1` - Banking / Insurance / Financial products / Credit Cards
  - `START 2` - Real Estate
  - `START 3` - Education
  - `START 4` - Health
  - `START 5` - Consumer goods and automobiles
  - `START 6` - Communication / Broadcasting / Entertainment / IT
  - `START 7` - Tourism and leisure
- **DND Activation Window:** DND preferences take effect within **24 hours** from submission.

---

### 2. Reporting Unsolicited Commercial Communication (UCC)
If you continue to receive promotional calls or spam from an unregistered number after activating DND:
1. Note the phone number, date, and time of the unsolicited call or message.
2. Send SMS to **1909** in the standard format:  
   `COMPLAINT <Description of spam>, <Spammer 10-digit number>, <DD/MM/YY>`  
   *(Example: `COMPLAINT Loan offer call, 9876543210, 24/10/26` to `1909`)*
3. The complaint must be registered within **3 days (72 hours)** of receiving the communication.
4. The offending sender will be served notice and their connection blacklisted upon repeated offense.

---

### 3. TeleNova AI Spam Shield
- Built-in machine learning network filter that automatically detects robocalls, spoofed sender IDs, and fraudulent SMS links.
- Suspicious calls display with a red `[Suspected Spam - TeleNova Shield]` badge on incoming screen.
- Free for all subscribers with no manual activation required.
