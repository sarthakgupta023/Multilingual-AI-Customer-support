### 2. Billing Cycle and Due Dates
- TeleNova generates bills on either the **1st**, **10th**, or **20th** of every calendar month depending on your activation circle.
- **Payment Due Date:** Exactly **18 days** from the bill generation date.
- **Late Payment Fee:** If unpaid by the due date, a late fee of ₹100 or 2% of the outstanding balance (whichever is higher) is applied to the subsequent bill.
- **Itemized Bill Request:** Itemized call detail records (CDR) are available free of cost in electronic PDF format on the TeleNova mobile app. Physical copies sent via post incur a processing charge of ₹50 per statement.

---

### 3. Credit Limits and Outgoing Barring
- Every customer is assigned a Dynamic Credit Limit (DCL) based on credit verification during onboarding.
- When unbilled usage reaches **80% of the credit limit**, an alert SMS and email are dispatched.
- When unbilled usage reaches **100% of the credit limit**, outgoing calls and mobile data are temporarily suspended until an interim payment is made. Incoming calls remain active for 72 hours following limit breach.
