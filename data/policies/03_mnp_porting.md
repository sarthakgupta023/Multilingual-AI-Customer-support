# TeleNova Mobile Number Portability (MNP) Guidelines

## Document ID: TN-POL-03
**Last Updated:** October 2026  
**Applicability:** Customers porting into or out of TeleNova

---

### 1. Eligibility Criteria for Porting
Per TRAI regulations:
- The mobile connection must have been active with the current donor operator for at least **90 days**.
- There must be no outstanding unbilled charges or pending bills on postpaid connections.
- The number must not be subject to any pending ownership transfers or legal prohibitions.
- A previous porting request must not have been executed within the last 90 days.

---

### 2. How to Generate Unique Porting Code (UPC)
1. Send an SMS containing the text: `PORT <Your 10-digit Mobile Number>` to **1900**.
   *(Example: `PORT 9876543210` to `1900`)*
2. In Jammu & Kashmir licensed circle, post-paid subscribers must dial `1900` instead of SMS.
3. You will receive an 8-character Unique Porting Code (UPC) via SMS from `1901`.
4. **UPC Validity:** The UPC remains valid for **4 calendar days** from the time of issue across all circles, except in Jammu & Kashmir, Assam, and North East licensed areas where it is valid for **30 calendar days**.

---

### 3. Porting into TeleNova: Required Steps & Documents
1. Visit any authorized TeleNova Store or schedule home delivery of your TeleNova SIM on the website.
2. Present the following:
   - Valid Unique Porting Code (UPC).
   - Original Aadhaar Card for instant biometric e-KYC (or Passport / Voter ID + passport photograph for physical KYC).
3. **Porting Timeline:** 
   - Within the same telecom circle (intra-circle): Completion within **3 working days**.
   - Between different telecom circles (inter-circle): Completion within **5 working days**.
   - Porting in Jammu & Kashmir, Assam, and North East takes up to **15 working days**.
4. Service disruption during the actual port-in transition happens between **10:00 PM and 5:00 AM** and lasts at most 4 hours.
