### 2. High-Speed 4G/5G Data Add-on Packs
When your daily quota runs out, high-speed data stops until midnight or until you recharge with a Data Booster:
- **₹19 Booster:** 1 GB data, validity existing plan.
- **₹29 Booster:** 2 GB data, validity existing plan.
- **₹65 Booster:** 4 GB data, validity existing plan.
- **₹181 Work-From-Home Pack:** 30 GB bulk data (no daily limit), valid for 30 days.

---

### 3. Voice Calling Specifics
- All plans include **free unlimited voice calls** to any network in India (Local and National STD).
- Voice calls while roaming on any network in India are completely free of charge.
- International calling (ISD) is **not included** in prepaid unlimited plans. Standard ISD rates apply unless an ISD pack is purchased.

---

### 4. Post-Quota Data Speeds
Once the daily high-speed limit is exhausted:
- Speed throttles to **64 kbps**.
- Standard messaging apps (WhatsApp text, email text) will continue to function.
- High-speed data resets automatically at **12:00:01 AM (Midnight)**.
