### 2. Hardware and Installation
- **Standard Installation Charge:** ₹1,000 for monthly billing cycles.
- **Free Installation Offer:** Installation and optical fiber drop cable setup are 100% free if the customer opts for a **3-month, 6-month, or 12-month advance booking**.
- **Wi-Fi 6 Router & 4K Android Smart Box:** Provided on a refundable security deposit of ₹1,000, waived on 6-month or annual advance subscriptions.

---

### 3. TeleNova AirFiber (5G Fixed Wireless Access - FWA)
- Available in locations where physical fiber cabling has not yet reached.
- Uses dedicated outdoor 5G directional antenna + indoor Wi-Fi 6 mesh gateway.
- Plans match standard Fiber tariffs starting at ₹699/month (100 Mbps).
- Installation requires outdoor line-of-sight verification by an authorized TeleNova engineer.
