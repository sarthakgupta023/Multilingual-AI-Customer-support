# TeleNova Customer Complaints, Billing Disputes & Refund Policy

## Document ID: TN-POL-07
**Last Updated:** October 2026  
**Applicability:** All TeleNova Subscribers nationwide

---

### 1. Failed Recharges & Double Deductions
If payment is deducted from your bank or UPI app but the recharge does not reflect on your TeleNova account:
1. **Automated Reconciliation:** The automated banking gateway initiates reconciliation within **2 hours**.
2. **Double Deductions:** If charged twice for a single transaction, the secondary charge is automatically refunded to the original payment source within **3 to 5 banking business days**.
3. **Manual Claim:** If the recharge is not delivered within 4 hours, submit the 12-digit UPI Reference Number / Bank Transaction ID via TeleNova App or by texting `REFUND <TransactionID>` to **198**.

---

### 2. Service Dispute Resolution Timelines (Turnaround Time - TAT)
Per TeleNova Customer Charter:
- **Billing Inquiries & Reversal Requests:** Resolved within **48 hours**.
- **Network Coverage & Tower Issues:** Inspection and response within **3 working days**.
- **SIM Deactivation / Fault Disputes:** Resolved within **24 hours**.
- **Value Added Services (VAS) Unwanted Activation:** Cancellation and 100% refund of wrongfully deducted charges processed within **24 hours**.

---

### 3. Three-Tier Grievance Escalation
- **Level 1 (Customer Care Helpline):** Dial **198** (toll-free) or email `support@telenova.in`. A unique 9-digit Service Request (SR) number is provided via SMS.
- **Level 2 (Circle Appellate Authority):** If your complaint is not resolved within the promised TAT or you are dissatisfied with the resolution, escalate with your SR number to the Circle Appellate Officer at `appellate.<circle>@telenova.in` (Working hours: Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM).
- **Level 3 (Consumer Grievance Redressal Forum):** Under TRAI guidelines, appeal directly to the Telecom Ombudsman if unresolved after 30 days.
