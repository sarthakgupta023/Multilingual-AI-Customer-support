# TeleNova Recharge Methods, Payment Modes & Cashback Offers

## Document ID: TN-POL-11
**Last Updated:** October 2026  
**Applicability:** All TeleNova Prepaid Subscribers

---

### 1. Supported Recharge Channels
- **TeleNova Official App & Website:** Instant recharge via UPI, Debit/Credit Cards, Net Banking, and TeleNova Pay Wallet.
- **Third-Party Platforms:** Recharges processed via Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, Amazon Pay, and CRED are officially supported.
- **Offline Retailers & Scratch Cards:** Available at over 150,000 retail partner outlets nationwide in denominations of ₹50, ₹100, ₹200, and ₹500.

---

### 2. TeleNova Pay Wallet & Cashback Offers
- **5% Auto-Pay Cashback:** Setting up UPI Auto-Pay or Credit Card Standing Instruction for unlimited plans ₹599+ awards **5% cashback** directly credited to TeleNova Pay balance.
- **First Recharge (FRC) Bonus:** New subscribers receive ₹50 talktime bonus + 5 GB extra data on their first recharge above ₹299.
- TeleNova Pay wallet balance can be used to pay fiber bills, recharge secondary family numbers, or purchase international roaming boosters.

---

### 3. Grace Periods and Number Expiry
- **Active Plan Expiration:** Incoming calls continue for **7 days** following prepaid pack expiration (Grace Period 1).
- **Grace Period 2:** If no recharge is done for **30 days**, incoming and outgoing services are completely suspended.
- **Number Deactivation:** If a prepaid number maintains zero balance and no incoming/outgoing activity for **90 consecutive days**, the connection is permanently deactivated and returned to the national number recycling pool under TRAI regulations.
