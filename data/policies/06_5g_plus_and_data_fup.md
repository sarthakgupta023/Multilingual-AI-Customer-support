# TeleNova 5G Ultra & Fair Usage Policy (FUP)

## Document ID: TN-POL-06
**Last Updated:** October 2026  
**Applicability:** All TeleNova 5G enabled circles & subscribers

---

### 1. Unlimited 5G Ultra Eligibility
TeleNova provides **True Unlimited 5G Data** with no daily speed caps under the following conditions:
1. The subscriber is located within an active TeleNova 5G network coverage zone.
2. The smartphone device supports 5G Standalone (SA) / Non-Standalone (NSA) bands: n28 (700MHz), n78 (3500MHz), and n258 (26GHz).
3. The customer is recharged on any prepaid plan with value **₹299 or higher** (e.g., Super299, Super599, Super849), or active on any **Infinity Postpaid Plan**.
4. 5G feature is claimed through the **TeleNova App** under the "5G Ultra Welcome Offer" tile.

---

### 2. Fair Usage Policy (FUP) on 4G Data
1. Each plan has an assigned daily or monthly quota (e.g., 1.5 GB/day, 2 GB/day, or 75 GB/month).
2. Data usage is calculated in 1 KB billing increments.
3. Upon consuming 100% of the allocated daily 4G quota:
   - Data speeds throttle to **64 kbps**.
   - No extra monetary deduction occurs automatically (zero balance deduction guarantee).
   - High-speed allocation resumes at 00:00 hours.
4. **Weekend Data Rollover:** Subscribers on Super299, Super599, and all Infinity plans carry forward unused Monday–Friday data quota into Saturday and Sunday (capped at maximum 10 GB per weekend).
