# TeleNova KYC Guidelines, Reverification & SIM Limits

## Document ID: TN-POL-12
**Last Updated:** October 2026  
**Applicability:** New and existing TeleNova Subscribers

---

### 1. Mandatory Know Your Customer (KYC) Requirements
To acquire a new TeleNova SIM card:
- **Digital Aadhaar e-KYC:** Immediate paperless activation using biometric fingerprint or iris scan + Aadhaar OTP. Activation occurs within **30 minutes**.
- **Digital D-KYC (Non-Aadhaar):** Accepted proof of identity & address include:
  - Passport
  - Voter ID Card (EPIC)
  - Driving License
  - Government Employee Identity Card
  - Live photo taken via agent app with geo-tagging and timestamp. Activation occurs within **4 to 6 hours**.

---

### 2. Maximum SIM Ownership Limit
- Per Department of Telecommunications (DoT) directive, an individual subscriber can register a maximum of **9 mobile connections** across all telecom service providers in India (maximum **6 connections** for residents of Jammu & Kashmir, Assam, and North East licensed circles).
- Any attempt to activate connections beyond this ceiling will be rejected by the automated DoT TAFCOP validation portal.

---

### 3. Outstation Customer Verification
- Customers acquiring a SIM card outside their permanent residential state must submit proof of permanent address plus a valid local reference or local employer/educational institution verification.
- Telephonic confirmation is conducted with the local contact before outbound calling is unlocked.
