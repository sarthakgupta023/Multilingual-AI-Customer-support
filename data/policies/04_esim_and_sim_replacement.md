# TeleNova eSIM Conversion and SIM Replacement Policy

## Document ID: TN-POL-04
**Last Updated:** October 2026  
**Applicability:** TeleNova Prepaid & Postpaid Subscribers

---

### 1. Lost or Damaged Physical SIM Replacement
If your phone is lost, stolen, or your SIM card is damaged:
1. **Immediate Block:** Call customer care helpline **198** (toll-free from TeleNova) or **1800-835-3668** (from any operator) to block outgoing and incoming services immediately.
2. **Visit Store:** Visit your nearest TeleNova Express Store or Experience Center.
3. **Mandatory Verification:** The registered subscriber must present their original photo identification (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Driving License, or Passport).
4. **Replacement Fee:** ₹50 (inclusive of taxes).
5. **Activation Window:** The replacement SIM card activates within **2 to 4 hours** of biometric e-KYC verification.
6. **SMS Barring Rule:** Per telecom security guidelines, incoming and outgoing SMS services remain barred for **24 hours** after SIM replacement to prevent banking OTP fraud.

---

### 2. Converting Physical SIM to eSIM
TeleNova supports eSIM activation for compatible Apple iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, and Google Pixel devices:
1. Ensure your device is connected to a stable Wi-Fi network.
2. Send an SMS: `ESIM <registered email address>` to **121**.
   *(Example: `ESIM customer@example.com` to `121`)*
3. You will receive an SMS confirmation from `121` requesting consent.
4. Reply `1` via SMS within **60 minutes** to authenticate the request.
5. TeleNova will initiate an automated confirmation voice call to your number. Press `1` when prompted.
6. A unique QR code is dispatched to your registered email address within 2 hours.
7. Open device **Settings > Mobile Data / Cellular > Add eSIM / Add Data Plan**, scan the QR code, and complete installation.
8. The eSIM is fully active within **2 hours**. SMS services are barred for 24 hours post-conversion.
